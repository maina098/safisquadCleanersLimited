Frequently asked questions

Safi Squad Cleaning Services Limited delivers a wide range of fabric‑care services including dry cleaning, wet wash, steam press,  stain removal, darning, and special treatments. We service garments, home linen (bedsheets, duvets, towels, pillow covers), home furnishings (curtains, carpets, table cloths), and special items like car seats, mosquito nets, luggage, and tents.

Safi Squad Cleaning Services Limited employs a unique 9‑step SigmaCare™ process that includes:

  • Unique barcode tagging for each garment
  • Fabric-specific segregation
  • Strict adherence to wash‑care labels
  •  Sanitization using our own Steriwash process
  • Use of organic, eco‑friendly detergents and RO‑treated water
  • Multiple quality checks (3 stage)
  • Professional packaging, and
  • Steam‑based pressing and finishing tailored to fabrics

At Safi Squad Cleaning Services Limited, we offer doorstep pickup & delivery in locations such as Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Pune, Mumbai, Hyderabad via our mobile app or customer care on 1800 123 4664.
